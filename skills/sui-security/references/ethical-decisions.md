# Ethical decisions, accessibility and AI provenance

Analyze the concrete choice and affected people; do not issue an automatic ethical score.

## Decision record

State the proposed behavior, beneficiaries, people exposed to failure or incentives, evidence/uncertainty, alternatives (including not collecting or not shipping), expected harms/benefits, ability to contest/undo, decision owner and revisit signal. Consider vulnerable/nonusers and operators as well as customers. Map relevant ACM commitments such as avoiding harm, fairness/accessibility, privacy, honesty and professional competence to an actual action and verification; citing a code does not establish an ethical result. Verify source text at https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics when making a specific attribution.

If a critical uncertainty can harm people, pause the affected rollout, preserve minimal safe evidence and escalate through actual accountable channels within user authorization. Prepare the concrete issue and proposed remediation; do not send messages or disclose data merely because the workflow mentions escalation. Confirm whether mitigations address the cause and monitor affected groups after any authorized change.

## Worked attendance forecast

A forecast that predicts event attendance from social-media activity is a useful review case. Ask whether the software respects users' humanity, whether it amplifies positive or negative behavior, and how its quality affects other people. Assess the five flourishing domains: happiness/life satisfaction; mental/physical health; meaning/purpose; character/virtue; close relationships. These prompts organize reasoning and are not measured outputs of an invented scoring model.

Potential benefits are less food waste and better event capacity. Risks include surveillance, mistaken exclusion, pressure to perform socially, chilling participation and disparate effects on people with private or no accounts. Evidence missing includes consent, data necessity, predictive value versus a simple RSVP baseline and error consequences. Prefer voluntary RSVPs/aggregate historical counts first; if an optional model is still justified, use minimal purpose-bound data, explain limits, permit correction/withdrawal, test subgroup/error effects and prohibit punitive individual decisions without a justified separate process. Record residual uncertainty and a stop trigger.

## Harmful incentives and accessibility

Hidden-fee/seating flows can extract money by exploiting pressure and family constraints; an ethical review asks whether users understand the consequence and have a meaningful alternative. Accessibility failures can exclude people even when the happy-path interface works. Test relevant keyboard/screen-reader/error-recovery tasks and supported devices with representative people; use current technical standards and applicable legal facts for a real decision. Historic Domino's or airline cases motivate the question, not universal current legal conclusions. VW, Therac-25, Uber and social-media examples should separate verified incident facts from the ethical failure mechanism and proposed control.

## LLM confidentiality and rights

Record provider/model/version, terms date, training/retrieval source provenance, permitted input categories, retention/training settings, third-party tool recipients and expected output use. Verify current primary provider terms before a consequential ownership/confidentiality claim. Do not send employer/customer secrets just because an API key exists. A generated output can contain third-party material or unsupported attribution; keep source/permission review for the actual artifact. Prefer local or minimized synthetic data when it meets the task. Mark unknown training rights or provenance unknown; neither AI authorship nor model confidence clears copyright, patents or contracts.
